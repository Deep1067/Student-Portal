Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about the student portal.

🔑 How do I reset my password?

Click on “Forgot Password?” on the login page and follow the steps to reset your password using your registered email ID.

📝 How do I submit my assignments?

Go to the Assignments page, select your course, upload your task file, and click Submit before the due date.

📊 Where can I check my grades?

Navigate to the Grades page to see your latest exam results and marksheet.

📅 How do I know about upcoming events?

Check the Events page where all upcoming workshops, seminars, and activities are listed.

📞 How can I contact support?

Use the Contact page to send a message to our support team or call during office hours.
